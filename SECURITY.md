# Security Notes for inspire

- **Supported variants**: InsPIRe^0 (NoPacking), InsPIRe^1 (OnePacking), InsPIRe^2 (Seeded+Packed). These are the only production-supported modes.
- **Modulus-switched responses**: the `mod-switch-response` feature is default-off in this crate and enabled by the adapter and client workspaces, which mod-switch every served response to `MOD_SWITCH_TARGET_36BIT` (`2^36 - 2^20 + 1`) and serialize it through the response serializer at 36 bits per coefficient, 10,446 bytes for a 512-byte row. The noise gate charges the target's residue `q' mod p` to the modelled error, and the constant is chosen for its residue of 33: without that charge the gate's ratio is identical for two primes of one bit width, and a seeded KAT (`production_post_switch_error_kat_at_45_36_and_33_bits`) pins error 652 at this prime against 53,503 at the largest 36-bit NTT prime (residue 53,266). The decode margin is measured on the switched response after the serializer by `served_post_switch_noise_distribution` (`benches/packing_noise_measurement.rs`, run with `--release --features mod-switch-response -- --ignored`), 1,000 samples per width across 40 sessions: worst error 1,113 against a decode boundary of 524,272 at 512-byte rows (gamma = 256), 8.88 bits of margin, and 417 at 32-byte rows (gamma = 16), 10.30 bits. It is taken across sessions because a session's packing key puts a fixed offset on every response of that session, so a margin taken inside one session reads a few tenths of a bit high. The modulus arrives off the wire, and `extract_inspiring_mod_switched` refuses, before deriving anything from it, any response modulus that is neither the CRS's own limbs nor an implemented target (45-bit, 36-bit). The checked 45-bit target passes the gate and the byte-identity corpus and is not served; the byte-aligned RIMS codec is not on the wire.
- **Rejected target**: the 33-bit prime `8,589,905,921` fails the production-cell noise gate at 0.57x. It is not exported and survives only in a test-only KAT.
- **Packed a-side caveat**: At d=256, gamma=4, bounded dense and structured recovery attempts against independently generated actual responses failed. Failure is not a privacy proof; production d=2048 and cross-key security bounds remain unmeasured.
- **Constant time in the NTT**: every `NttContext` transform (Montgomery, Solinas at `DEFAULT_Q`, Shoup), `pointwise_mul`, `pointwise_mul_single`, `pointwise_mul_single_at`, `pointwise_mul_shoup`, `pointwise_mul_solinas`, `to_mont`, `from_mont`, `solinas_mont_mul_default_q` and `pointwise_solinas_mont_mul` corrects by a `cmov` written in `asm!` on x86-64 and by a borrow mask elsewhere, with no source branch on a coefficient. `tests/secret_dependent_spelling_gate.rs` pins that spelling in each of these functions, the butterflies and the correction helpers, allowing the public modulus dispatch as their only branch; it is a source-pattern check, not a timing proof. Release codegen was inspected at rustc 1.98 and is not gated in CI: on x86-64 the conditional jumps in these functions test only loop counters, slice bounds and the public modulus dispatch; on wasm32 every correction is a `select` and every `br_if` tests a 32-bit index or the public modulus dispatch. Not covered: how a WebAssembly engine compiles `select`; the wasm32 128-bit multiply, a call into compiler-builtins' `__multi3` that was not inspected; native targets other than x86-64, which take the mask without inspection; the AVX-512 IFMA kernels, not re-inspected; `shoup_precompute_vec`, which divides its operand; twiddle setup, which sees only public values. Outside the NTT, see the next item.
- **Constant time in the client ring arithmetic**: `Poly` addition, subtraction and negation (`+`, `-`, unary `-`, `add_ntt_domain`, `add_assign_ntt_domain`) correct through the NTT's helper above. `Poly::scalar_mul` and `scalar_mul_assign` multiply each residue by a Shoup product with the public scalar, corrected by that helper; the only divides take the public scalar and modulus, and the only branch picks the remainder width from the public modulus (above `2^63` the remainder is held in 128 bits and corrected through `subtle`). The residue reduction behind `from_coeffs_moduli`, `from_crt_coeffs` and every constructor that reduces is Barrett over the public modulus with the same correction. `LweSecretKey::from_rlwe` composes a two-limb key without a branch or divide, and the tree-packed extractor unscales the decrypted record by a Shoup product with the public `d^-1`. With the NTT, the shard mapping, `inverse_monomial`, the key automorphism, the Gaussian lift and decryption already pinned, query generation (`query`, `query_seeded` and the `ClientSession` forms), RLWE and RGSW encryption, key-switching matrix generation and packing-key generation reach the secret key and the secret index only through these functions and through permutations by public index tables. `tests/secret_dependent_spelling_gate.rs` is a source-text check, not a proof: it extracts each pinned item body by its header and fails when the body contains a substring it denies or lacks one it requires, so it catches the spellings it lists and nothing else. It pins the query bodies, `RlweCiphertext::encrypt` and `encrypt_with_crs`, both RGSW `encrypt_with_rng`, `generate_ks_matrix`, `generate_ksk_body`, `extract_packed` and the ring arithmetic above (the reducing constructors through `reduce`, `reduce_residues` and `from_coeffs_moduli`), and requires each encryption and key-generation site among them to scale through `Poly::scalar_mul`. It denies the five query bodies (`query` and `query_seeded`, their `ClientSession` forms, and the body the seeded free function forwards to) every `/`, `%`, `[`, `if `, `for `, `while `, `loop` and spaced comparison operator (`<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`, `==`, `!=`); the substrings `div`, `rem`, `euclid`, `128`, `into(`, `from(`, `wrapping_`, `overflowing_`, `checked_`, `saturating_`, `select`, `then`, `min(`, `max(`, `clamp(`, `cmp(`, `.get(`, `nth(`, `skip(`, `split_at(` and `matches!`; and the method comparisons `.lt(`, `.le(`, `.gt(`, `.ge(`, `.eq(` and `.ne(`; and it allows the token `match ` only on the public session handle. None of these appears in the query bodies today. It scans every body it pins, after lifting a listed public branch and its `else` out of four of them, for `checked_sub(`, `checked_add(`, an `unwrap_or` fallback, `then_some(`, `.then(`, `.min(`, `.max(`, `} else {`, `else if ` and a spaced comparison inside `u64::from(...)` or `(...) as u64` (or any other primitive integer type); a pinned body left out of that scan fails the gate, matched by source and header so that dropping one of two items sharing a header fails too. Not covered by the gate: outside the query bodies, a bare `if` with no `else` passes unless that body's own pin denies `if `, and a comparison bound to a `let` before its cast to an integer passes unless that body's pin denies every comparison operator; any spelling it does not list, such as a widening through a type alias; and any spelling moved into a helper it does not pin. The codegen inspection is the stronger evidence and is not gated in CI. Release codegen was inspected at rustc 1.98: on x86-64 the inner loops of the scalar product, the sum, difference and negation, the reduction and `from_rlwe` take one `cmov` or a `subtle` select per coefficient and jump only on loop counters, slice bounds and the public width dispatch, and their only `div` instructions and `__udivti3` calls take the public scalar, modulus or reciprocal; on wasm32 each correction is a `select` or a `subtle` mask. The exclusions of the NTT item apply here too, plus: `Poly::coeff` on a two-limb polynomial and `Poly::set_coeff` still branch and divide (no client path reaches them with a secret: extractors read single-limb plaintexts); `mul_acc_ntt_domain` and `mul_acc3_ntt_domain` still branch, on the server's public operands; `linf_norm`, `l2_norm_squared` and `is_zero` branch on coefficients and are diagnostics; the LWE path (`LweCiphertext::encrypt`, `decrypt` and the stateless `ModQ` arithmetic) still divides and branches and is not on the served path; `extract_with_tolerance` branches on the decrypted value; the tight codec's canonical-form check branches on each coefficient of a serialized secret key, an outcome fixed for any valid key; and the Gaussian sampler's trip count varies, independently of the sample (see `src/math/gaussian.rs`).
- **Reporting issues**: Please open a GitHub issue in this repository with a minimal reproduction or description. Avoid including sensitive data in issue text.
